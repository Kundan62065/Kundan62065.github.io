SentinelFund Smart Contract Threat Matrix

Solidity 0.8.20 • STRIDE Vulnerability Analysis & Mitigations

1. SWC-107: Reentrancy Attack Protection

Threat: Malicious contract fallback functions recursively invoking withdrawal functions prior to balance deduction.

Mitigation: Strict Checks-Effects-Interactions (CEI) where contributions[msg.sender] = 0; occurs before external transfer + custom nonReentrant mutex guard.

2. SWC-105: Rug-Pull & Centralization Risk

Threat: Single campaign creator withdrawing 100% of the funds anonymously upon campaign closure.

Mitigation: Elimination of unilateral withdraw(); milestone release requires decentralized backer quorum voting (approvalCount > contributorCount / 2).

3. SWC-113: Denial of Service via Failed Call

Threat: Looping over contributor arrays during mass refunds fails if one address reverts or gas limit is exceeded.

Mitigation: Pull-over-Push payments pattern. Backers call claimRefund() individually, isolating transactions.

4. Circuit Breaker Emergency Control

Threat: Zero-day protocol anomaly draining funds before patch deployment.

Mitigation: Emergency isPaused toggle halting contributions, votes, and disbursements during active incidents.
